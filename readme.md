Mixed logit for residential preferences
The code reports the aggregated data to individual observations that report:
- category: joint category (ethnicity x NS-SEC) of the person
- homo_ethnic: the proportion of members of the own ethnic group in the borough of the citizen for that wave
- homo_sc: the proportion of members of the own SC class in the borough of the citizen for that wave
- homo_group: the proportion of members of the own category (ethnicity x NS-SEC) of the citizen in the borough for that wave
- borough_year: borough and year of residence as nominal
- borough and year: the nominal borough and numeric year
